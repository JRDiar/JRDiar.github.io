Implementation Plan - Entry Validation Improvements (Bug #17)
Goal Description
Enhance the bot's "Patience" by strictly validating the development of the price action before entry. This prevents premature entries on the first sign of force by ensuring:

Structural Readiness: The ZigZag structure is in a "Rebound" (HL/LH) or "Start" (L/H) phase, NOT at a "Top/Bottom" (H/HH/L/LL).
Momentum Maturity: Momentum has at least 2 candles of development, regardless of ADX strength.
Active Bounce Check: The price is not actively bouncing against our direction (e.g., 3 green candles when trying to short near active support).
